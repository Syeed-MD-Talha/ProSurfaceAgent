## Pricing model (critical)

- No fixed price list is published on the website.
- Real sales flow (see whatsapp-job-flow skill) charges a $40 inspection fee for the site visit. The quotation after inspection is free. Never say "free inspection".
- Pricing depends on service, area size, and extent of damage. After inspection, the team quotes that night (e.g. parquet repair $430 + sanding/varnish $410 = $840 subtotal in the real transcript — an example, not a price list).
- Deposit pattern is 50 percent of the quoted amount with postal-code + unit as payment reference (e.g. $420 advance on $840, ref 128045 0405).

Share photos and location for a free assessment, usually acknowledged within about 10 minutes in operating hours."

## How it works

1. Free Quote. Send details by WhatsApp, email, or contact form. Usually acknowledged within about 10 minutes during operating hours.
2. Site Visit and Booking. Assessment confirms scope and price, visits typically within around 5 days.
3. The Work. Most single-room jobs take 2 to 4 days. Larger commercial or multi-room projects scheduled after assessment.
4. Payment and Warranty. 40 percent on confirmation, 30 percent at major works, 30 percent on completion, by bank transfer or PayNow. Workmanship covered 6 to 12 months depending on service.

## What to send for a quote

Location, floor or surface type, description of issue, photos where possible, preferred timing. Photos give an initial view before confirming if a full site visit is needed.

## Singapore guidance used on site

- Restore, repair, or replace? Dull but sound -> polish or refinish. Etching, scratches, stains -> restoration. Few loose or cracked pieces -> localised repair. Widespread structural failure -> replacement may be practical, confirmed on-site.
- Humidity, heat, grit from corridors and car parks, timber movement between air-conditioned and open areas all affect floors. Maintenance interval depends on use, not a fixed schedule.
