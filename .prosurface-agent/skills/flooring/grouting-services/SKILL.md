# Grouting Services

Regrouting, marble gum grouting, and epoxy grouting for tiled and stone surfaces in Singapore homes and businesses.

Source pages: https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/services/grouting/ and subpages, https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/faq/

## When to use

Use when the user asks about grout: cracked, dark, mouldy, missing, loose, bathroom or kitchen grout, regrouting, marble gum, epoxy grout.

## Services in this category

1. Tile and Marble Regrouting (/services/grouting/tile-marble-regrouting/) — removes old cement-based grout where needed and replaces it. For cracked, discoloured, or deteriorated standard grout.
2. Marble Gum Grouting (/services/grouting/marble-gum-grouting/) — replaces old grout with marble gum for a seamless, glossy, marble-like finish. Typically on homogeneous, porcelain, or granite tiles where a smoother uniform joint is wanted. Different technique from standard regrouting.
3. Epoxy Grouting (/services/grouting/epoxy-grouting/) — waterproof, stain-resistant material upgrade. Generally for kitchens, bathrooms, wet areas, or new installations. A material upgrade, not a like-for-like repair.

## Decision guide

- Grout dirty but sound -> cleaning is usually enough.
- Grout cracked, missing, loose, crumbling -> replacement.
- Want seamless glossy joint on homogeneous / porcelain / granite -> marble gum.
- Wet area or new install, want waterproof and stain-resistant -> epoxy.
- Damaged grout can generally be renewed without replacing surrounding tiles.

## Common problems and answers

- "Bathroom grout turning dark?" Mould, moisture, soap in porous grout. Needs cleaning or replacing, not just scrubbing.
- "Grout cracking or crumbling?" End of lifespan, remove and replace.
- "Missing lines?" Exposes edges to water, address promptly.
- "Loose or hollow?" Failed bond, replace.
- "Musty smell near tiles?" Often moisture trapped in damaged grout.
- "How long does regrouting take?" Depends on area and removal needed. Most bathroom or kitchen jobs complete within a few days.

## Process

1. Inspect existing grout for cracking, staining, looseness, missing sections.
2. Identify cause: age, moisture, material mismatch.
3. Select system: cement-based, marble gum, or epoxy, based on tile and problem.
4. Complete work and check finish. Mapei and Sika products are used, selected per job.
