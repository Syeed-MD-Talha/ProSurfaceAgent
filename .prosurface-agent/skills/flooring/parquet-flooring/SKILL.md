# Parquet Flooring

New installation plus polishing, sanding, varnishing, repair, staircase refinishing, old varnish removal, and skirting for parquet floors in Singapore homes and commercial spaces.

Source pages: https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/services/parquet-flooring/ and subpages including /parquet-floor-polishing/, https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/faq/

## When to use

Use when the user asks about parquet: dull parquet, scratches, sanding, varnishing, repair, installation, staircase, skirting, old varnish, glossy vs matte finish.

## Services in this category

1. Parquet Flooring Installation (/services/parquet-flooring/parquet-flooring-installation/) — new floors: assess suitability, choose material, prepare subfloor, install.
2. Parquet Floor Polishing (/services/parquet-flooring/parquet-floor-polishing/) — for existing parquet that lost shine but needs no sanding. Steps: assessment, surface preparation, polishing with method matched to condition, finish refinement, final inspection. Maintains existing finish.
3. Parquet Repair and Restoration (/services/parquet-flooring/parquet-repair-restoration/) — for warped, loose, cracked, or missing planks. Localised repair often avoids full refinish.
4. Parquet Floor Sanding and Varnishing (/services/parquet-flooring/parquet-floor-sanding-varnishing/) — full refinish: sand back, then new varnish. For worn or deteriorated finish.
5. Staircase Sanding and Varnishing (/services/parquet-flooring/staircase-sanding-varnishing/) — same refinish process for staircases, which wear differently.
6. Old Varnish Removal (/services/parquet-flooring/old-varnish-removal/) — strip peeled, yellowed, or failed varnish before refinishing. Sanding over failed finish will not last.
7. Skirting Installation and Repair (/services/parquet-flooring/skirting-installation-repair/) — skirting alongside new parquet or repair of loose or damaged boards.

## Decision guide

- Dull, intact surface, needs shine refresh -> polishing.
- Worn finish, needs bare-wood prep + new coat -> sanding + varnishing.
- Peeling, yellowing, flaking varnish -> remove old varnish first.
- Warped, loose, cracked, missing pieces -> repair and restoration.
- New floor -> installation.
- Ronseal and Nippon finishes are used for varnishing, matched to matte or glossy choice. Lagler Hummel 8-inch sander is used for parquet sanding.

## Common questions

- "Old parquet, restore?" Usually yes. Polishing vs sanding vs repair depends on condition, confirmed on-site.
- "Polish or sand?" Polish suits lost shine. Sanding suits finish that must be taken back.
- "How often to refinish?" Depends on traffic, sun, maintenance. Heavily used areas sooner.
- "Damaged blocks?" Warped, loose, cracked, missing planks can usually be repaired individually.
- "How long does polishing take?" Depends on area size and condition, confirmed after assessment.
- Maintenance: use parquet-suitable products, avoid excess moisture and abrasives, protect from furniture scratches.

## Singapore context

Timber moves with humidity and temperature shifts between air-conditioned rooms and open humid corridors. Boards can loosen and varnish can wear. Assessment is on the actual floor, not a fixed timeline.
