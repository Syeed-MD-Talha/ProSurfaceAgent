# General Floor Care

Polishing, buffing, refinishing, and restoration for non-marble hard floors: homogeneous tile, granite, terrazzo, and concrete. For residential and commercial floors across Singapore.

Source pages: https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/services/general-floor-care/ and subpages, https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/faq/

## When to use

Use when the user asks about homogeneous tiles, granite floors, terrazzo, concrete, dull tile glaze, scuffs, polishing vs buffing vs refinishing. Do NOT use for marble floors (see marble-stone-care) or parquet (see parquet-flooring).

## Services in this category

1. Floor Polishing and Buffing (/services/general-floor-care/floor-polishing-buffing/) — for floors dulled or scuffed but with a workable finish underneath. Restores shine without a full refinish.
2. Floor Refinishing and Restoration (/services/general-floor-care/floor-refinishing-restoration/) — for finish worn through, deeper scratches, staining, or unevenness that polishing alone will not correct.

## Decision guide

- Finish largely intact, just lost shine -> polishing.
- Finish in reasonable condition, light maintenance -> buffing (not every floor suits buffing).
- Finish worn through or deeper damage -> refinishing.
- Homogeneous tile dulled glaze -> usually polishing.
- Light scratches and scuffs -> often polish out. Deeper scratches -> refinishing.

## Materials covered

- Homogeneous tiles: common in HDB and condo units, wear shows as dulled or scratched glaze.
- Granite: dense and hard-wearing, still loses finish in high-traffic commercial areas.
- Terrazzo: common in older properties and common areas, often needs grinding and polishing to bring back aggregate finish.
- Concrete: warehouses, car parks, some commercial units, typically refinished rather than polished to high shine.

## Process

1. Identify floor material.
2. Check surface condition and existing finish.
3. Recommend polishing, buffing, or refinishing. Confirmed on-site, not guessed by phone.
4. Carry out with Klindex planetary grinding and polishing equipment, level matched to surface.
