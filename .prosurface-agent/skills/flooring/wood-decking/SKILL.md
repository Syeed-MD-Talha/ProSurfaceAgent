# Wood Decking

Outdoor timber decking for balconies, patios, and landed properties in Singapore. New installation plus repair and restoration of weathered decks.

Source pages: https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/services/wood-decking/ and /wood-deck-installation-repair/, https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/faq/

## When to use

Use when the user asks about outdoor decks, balcony timber, patio decking, faded or weathered decks, deck repair vs replacement. For indoor timber or parquet floors, use other skills.

## Scope

- New decking: timber selection, subframe and surface preparation, installation.
- Existing decking: repair for damaged boards, restoration for faded, worn, or weathered but structurally usable decks.
- Both start with on-site assessment.

## Signs a deck needs attention

Faded colour, greyed weathered look, splinters or rough surface, cracked or split boards, worn finish, staining from water or leaves, loose sections near edges or joints, heavier wear in some areas.

## Decision guide

- Damage limited, boards usable, wear mainly finish-related -> repair or restoration (clean, sand, refinish, or replace individual boards).
- Damage extensive, structure compromised, large sections deteriorated -> replacement of sections may be more practical, confirmed on-site.
- Structurally sound surface issue -> sanding and refinishing usually enough.
- Fading alone is typically surface and finish, usually addressed through cleaning, sanding, and refinishing.

## Care

Regular cleaning, clearing debris between boards, and addressing wear early reduces later work. Finish condition protects the timber underneath, not just appearance.

## Singapore context

Outdoor timber faces year-round moisture and strong sun. Finish and boards are assessed as ongoing care, not only when problems are obvious. Sheltered decks need attention later than full-sun decks.
