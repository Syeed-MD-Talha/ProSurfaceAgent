# Vinyl Flooring

SPC and LVT material guidance, installation, and care for existing vinyl floors showing damage or wear. For homes and offices across Singapore.

Use when the user asks about vinyl, SPC, LVT, vinyl installation, vinyl repair, lifting vinyl, scratches, vinyl over tiles, vinyl maintenance.

## Material guide

- SPC (stone plastic composite): more rigid, water-resistant. Generally suits higher moisture or heavier daily use.
- LVT (luxury vinyl tile): more flexible, often chosen for appearance and feel. Tends to suit lower-traffic residential rooms.
- Choice depends on area of use, foot traffic, subfloor condition, and look and feel. Vinyl handles humidity better than timber, but subfloor condition still matters.

## Installation

Assess existing floor, prepare subfloor, take accurate measurements, fit with method suited to material and space. Can sometimes be installed over existing tiles depending on condition, level, and stability, confirmed during assessment. A level, sound surface gives a better result since thin vinyl can show irregularities.

## Repair vs replacement

- Repair may fit when: damage limited, section addressable on its own, surrounding flooring still usable. Examples: local tear, lifted section, scratches, depending on product and visibility.
- Replacement may fit when: damage extensive, multiple areas affected, flooring at end of lifespan. Repair on a failing floor is usually not practical.
- Vinyl suits bedrooms, living areas, offices, retail, indoor commercial interiors where durable low-maintenance floor is wanted.

## Common problems

- "Section lifting." Often moisture underneath or weakened adhesive, usually worsens if left.
- "Scratches near entrance." High-traffic wear, normal but fixable.
- "Torn or split." Sharp impact or heavy furniture. Patch depends on location and size.
- "Patchy or faded colour." Sun and uneven wear, more noticeable on some types.

## Care

Regular cleaning, prompt removal of dirt and spills, follow manufacturer guidance for the specific product. Address lifting or scratches early.
