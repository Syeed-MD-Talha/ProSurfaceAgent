# Marble and Stone Care

Polishing and restoration for marble, granite, and other stone surfaces on floors, kitchen countertops, and bathroom vanity or basin tops. For homes and businesses across Singapore.

Source pages: https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/services/marble-stone-care/ and subpages, https://www.prosurfaceworks.com/faq/

## When to use

Use when the user asks about marble floors, stone floors, granite, countertops, vanity tops, dull marble, scratches, etching, stains, or whether marble needs polishing vs restoration.

## Services in this category

1. Marble Floor Polishing (/services/marble-stone-care/marble-floor-polishing/) — for marble floors dulled from everyday wear but not otherwise damaged. Restores shine without addressing structural issues.
2. Marble Restoration (/services/marble-stone-care/marble-restoration/) — for cracks, etching, or deeper staining that polishing alone will not fix.
3. Kitchen Countertop Polishing (/services/marble-stone-care/kitchen-countertop-polishing/) — for marble, granite, or solid-surface countertops scratched, dulled, or stained from daily use.
4. Vanity and Basin Top Polishing (/services/marble-stone-care/vanity-basin-top-polishing/) — for bathroom vanity tops exposed to water, soap residue, and toiletries.

## Decision guide

- Dull from everyday wear, no damage -> polishing.
- Scratches, etching, cracks, deep stains -> restoration first, then refinish.
- Countertop / vanity dull or lightly scratched -> polishing, adjusted for material and use.
- Granite and terrazzo FLOORS, homogeneous tile, concrete -> NOT this category, use general-floor-care skill.

## Common problems and answers

- "My marble floor lost its shine." Usually surface wear or residue, often solved with polishing.
- "Acidic spill left a dull patch." This is etching, needs restoration, not just cleaning.
- "Stone looks stained." Depends on cause and age, confirmed on-site.
- "High-traffic spots look worse." Common near entrances and hallways.
- "Can scratched marble be polished?" Fine scratches and dullness often improve; deep scratching depends on depth.
- "Countertops?" Yes, kitchen and vanity tops are polished on the same principles as floors.
- "How often?" Depends on traffic, location, and cleaning. No fixed schedule.

## Process

1. Assess the surface on-site.
2. Identify cause: wear, etching, staining, or structural damage.
3. Recommend polishing, restoration, or a targeted treatment.
4. Carry out work with equipment matched to stone and finish. Klindex machines are used for deep restoration and high-gloss planetary polishing.
