What Is Parquet Floor Polishing?
Parquet floor polishing is a professional treatment applied to an existing parquet surface, aimed at improving its appearance and finish. The specific treatment used depends on the floor's existing condition.
When Should You Consider Parquet Floor Polishing?
The floor looks dull
It has lost the shine it once had
There is light surface wear
The finish looks uneven
High-traffic areas look noticeably different from the rest of the floor
The floor no longer has the appearance it used to
What Can Professional Parquet Polishing Improve?
Surface appearance
Shine
Smoothness
Consistency of the finish
The overall visual condition of the floor
Polishing does not remove every scratch, make old parquet look brand new, or produce a permanent shine. The final result depends on the floor's existing condition and finish.
Parquet Polishing vs Sanding & Varnishing
Polishing
Generally appropriate for refreshing appearance, improving shine, addressing lighter surface wear, and maintaining an existing floor finish.
Sanding & Varnishing
A more extensive treatment involving surface sanding, removal or refinement of the existing finish where appropriate, and application of a new varnish or finish.

Does Your Parquet Need Polishing or Restoration?
Polishing may be appropriate when:
The main issue is dullness

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is parquet floor polishing?
Parquet floor polishing is a professional treatment applied to an existing parquet surface to improve its appearance and finish.
Can polishing remove scratches from parquet flooring?
Light surface scratches may improve through polishing. Deeper scratches or damage typically need repair, restoration or sanding instead.
How often should parquet flooring be polished?
This depends on usage and traffic. Our team can advise on a suitable interval after assessing your floor.
What is the difference between polishing and sanding?
Polishing refreshes the existing finish. Sanding is a more extensive treatment that removes and renews the finish. See
Parquet Floor Sanding & Varnishing
for more.
Can old parquet flooring be polished?
In many cases, yes, provided the underlying parquet is in a suitable condition. This is confirmed during assessment.
How long does parquet floor polishing take?
This depends on the size of the area and the floor's condition, and is confirmed after the assessment.
