When Does Tile or Marble Grouting Need to Be Replaced?
Grout has cracked or broken along the joint lines
Sections of grout are missing
Grout feels loose or crumbles when touched or cleaned
Grout has discoloured and does not respond to cleaning
There is persistent staining along the joints
Gaps have opened up between tiles or marble pieces
The grout has become difficult to keep clean
These signs relate to the condition of the grout itself, which is a separate matter from the condition of the tiles or marble around it.
What Is Tile & Marble Regrouting?
Regrouting involves removing deteriorated existing grout from the joints between tiles or marble and replacing it with suitable new grout.
Removing the existing, deteriorated grout
Preparing the joints for new material
Applying new grout suited to the surface and location
Finishing the joints and cleaning the surrounding surface
Signs Your Tiles or Marble May Need Regrouting
Cracked joints
Grout lines that are visibly cracked or broken along their length.
Missing grout
Sections where grout is absent, leaving visible gaps between tiles or marble.
Loose or crumbling grout
Grout that deteriorates further during normal cleaning or light contact.
Persistent discolouration
Grout that stays discoloured even after regular cleaning.
Open joints
Gaps that have formed between tiles or marble, with the surface condition worsening around them.
Does Your Grout Need Cleaning or Regrouting?
Cleaning may be suitable when:
The grout is structurally intact
The main issue is surface dirt
Discolouration is primarily superficial
Regrouting may be considered when:
The grout is cracked
Grout is missing in places
Joints have deteriorated
Existing grout cannot be effectively restored through cleaning
Regrouting Tiles and Marble
Tile Regrouting
Tile regrouting is commonly carried out in bathrooms, kitchens, floors and walls, wherever the existing grout has deteriorated.
Marble Regrouting
Marble regrouting addresses grout joints in marble flooring and wall surfaces, with the grout and method matched to the marble itself rather than a generic tile approach.
How Tile & Marble Regrouting Works
1. Surface Assessment
The tiles or marble, along with the condition of the existing grout, are assessed.
2. Existing Grout Removal
Deteriorated grout is carefully removed from the joints.
3. Joint Preparation
The joints are cleaned and prepared for new grout.
4. New Grout Application
Suitable grout is applied to the prepared joints.
5. Finishing & Cleaning
Excess grout is removed, joints are finished, and the surrounding surface is cleaned.

Regrouting for Bathrooms and Wet Areas
Bathroom floors, shower areas and other wet zones are common places where grout deteriorates, since these joints are regularly exposed to moisture.
Properly maintained grout joints can help maintain the condition of tiled or marble surfaces, but regrouting alone does not resolve underlying waterproofing failures. Where a waterproofing issue is suspected, that needs to be assessed separately from the grout itself.
Tile & Marble Regrouting in Singapore
Frequently used bathrooms, wet areas and high-traffic tiled floors in Singapore homes and commercial properties are common candidates for regrouting, particularly where grout has not been maintained or replaced for some time.
When Should You Consider More Than Regrouting?
Regrouting addresses the grout joints themselves, but it is not the right solution for every underlying issue. It may not be enough on its own if:
Tiles are loose
Tiles are cracked
The underlying substrate has issues
A waterproofing problem is suspected
There is significant structural movement
In these cases, a proper assessment is needed to identify what is actually causing the problem before deciding on the appropriate work.

Use cleaning products suitable for the grout type

Keep an eye on joints in wet areas in particular
FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I know if my tile grout needs replacing?
Cracked, missing, crumbling or persistently discoloured grout are the main signs. If cleaning no longer improves the appearance or condition, regrouting is usually the next step.
Can old grout be removed and replaced?
Yes, existing deteriorated grout can be removed and replaced with new grout suited to the surface and location.
Can marble be regrouted?
Yes, marble regrouting is carried out on marble flooring and wall surfaces, with the grout and approach matched to the marble.
Is regrouting better than cleaning grout?
It depends on the grout's condition. If the grout is structurally intact and just dirty, cleaning may be enough. If it is cracked, missing or deteriorated, regrouting is generally the more effective option.
Does regrouting fix leaks?
Regrouting addresses the condition of the grout joints. It does not resolve underlying waterproofing failures, which need to be assessed and addressed separately.
How long does tile and marble regrouting take?
This depends on the area involved and the condition of the existing grout, and is confirmed after the assessment.
