Marble & Stone Care
Can scratched marble floors be polished?
In many cases, yes. Fine scratches and dullness can often be improved through polishing, though the result depends on the depth of the scratching and the marble's condition. See
Marble Floor Polishing
What is the difference between marble polishing and marble restoration?
Polishing addresses routine dullness and light-to-medium wear, while restoration addresses more significant issues such as cracks, etching or deep staining. See
Marble Restoration
for the more extensive treatment.
Can marble countertops be polished?
Yes, marble kitchen countertops can be polished to address dullness and light surface wear. See
Kitchen Countertop Polishing
Can stains or dullness be removed from marble?
This depends on the type and depth of the staining. Surface-level dullness generally responds well to polishing, while deeper or set-in staining may need a more involved restoration approach.
How do I know whether my marble needs polishing or restoration?
Routine dullness or loss of shine usually points to polishing, while visible damage such as cracks, etching or deep staining points to restoration. An assessment confirms which applies to your floor.
Grouting Services
When should tile or marble grout be replaced?
Grout is typically replaced when it has cracked, crumbled, discoloured significantly or come away from the tile edges. See
Tile & Marble Regrouting
What is the difference between regrouting and epoxy grouting?
Regrouting replaces existing grout with standard cement-based grout, while epoxy grouting uses a waterproof, stain-resistant material, usually for wet areas or new installations. See
Epoxy Grouting
Can damaged grout be repaired without replacing the tiles?
Yes, grout work addresses the grout lines themselves rather than the tiles, so damaged or deteriorated grout can generally be renewed without needing to replace the surrounding tiles.
What is marble gum grouting used for?
Marble gum grouting replaces old grout with marble gum to create a seamless, glossy, marble-like finish, typically on homogeneous, porcelain or granite tiles. See
Marble Gum Grouting
General Floor Care
What is the difference between floor polishing and buffing?
Polishing and buffing are both routine surface-care treatments for hard floors such as homogeneous tile, granite, terrazzo and concrete, aimed at refreshing shine rather than fixing deeper damage. See
Floor Polishing & Buffing
When does a floor need polishing?
Polishing is generally suitable when a floor has become dull or lost its shine but isn't showing deeper cracks, etching or structural wear.
Can floor polishing remove scratches?
Light surface scratches can often be improved through polishing, though the result depends on how deep the scratching is and the floor's overall condition.
Can an old floor be restored instead of replaced?
In many cases, yes, particularly where the wear is on the surface rather than structural. See
Floor Refinishing & Restoration
for more significant wear.
Parquet Flooring
Can damaged parquet flooring be repaired?
In many cases, yes, particularly where the damage is localised and the surrounding boards remain sound. See
Parquet Repair & Restoration
When does parquet flooring need polishing?
Polishing is generally suitable when the existing parquet has become dull or lost its shine, rather than showing physical damage. See
Parquet Floor Polishing
What is involved in parquet floor sanding and varnishing?
It involves preparing and sanding the existing surface, then applying a new or renewed varnish finish, addressing a worn or deteriorated finish rather than physical damage. See
Parquet Floor Sanding & Varnishing
Can old varnish be removed from parquet flooring?
Yes, old or deteriorated varnish can be addressed and removed to prepare the surface for its next treatment. See
Old Varnish Removal
Can parquet flooring be restored without replacing the entire floor?
Often, yes. Where damage is localised, repair or restoration can address specific sections without requiring a full floor replacement. The extent of the damage confirms which approach applies.
Timber & Wood Repair
What types of timber floor damage can be repaired?
This depends on the item and the extent of the damage. Cracks, warping, loose joints and surface damage on non-flooring wood items such as doors, furniture and cabinets are commonly addressed.
Can damaged timber boards be replaced individually?
In many cases, individual damaged components can be replaced without needing to replace the whole item, depending on the extent of the damage and the availability of matching material.
How do I know whether timber flooring needs repair or replacement?
This depends on how widespread the damage is and the condition of the surrounding wood. If your question relates to parquet flooring specifically, see
Parquet Repair & Restoration
; for other wood items such as doors, furniture or cabinets, see
Timber & Wood Repair
Wood Decking
Can damaged wood decking be repaired?
In many cases, yes, particularly where the damage is localised. More extensive or widespread damage may call for more significant work. See
Wood Deck Installation & Repair
Can you install a new wood deck?
Yes, we install new outdoor wood decks for residential and commercial spaces, based on the site's layout, exposure and intended use. See
Wood Deck Installation & Repair
How do I know whether a deck needs repair or replacement?
Repair is generally suitable when damage is localised and the supporting structure remains sound. Replacement is considered when damage is widespread or repeated repairs are no longer practical.
What can affect the condition of outdoor wood decking?
Singapore's outdoor conditions, including humidity, rainfall and sun exposure, can cause weathering, discolouration, cracking, warping and, in some conditions, biological deterioration over time.
Do you provide wood decking services for residential properties?
Yes, we install and repair wood decking for landed homes, condominiums and other private outdoor areas, as well as suitable commercial properties.
Vinyl Flooring
Can vinyl flooring be installed over existing tiles?
In some cases, yes, but this depends on the condition, level and stability of the existing tiles, which is confirmed during a site assessment. See
Vinyl Flooring Installation
Does vinyl flooring require a level subfloor?
A level, sound surface generally gives a better result, since vinyl is relatively thin and can show irregularities beneath it. The amount of preparation needed depends on the existing floor's condition.
Is vinyl flooring suitable for residential properties?
Yes, vinyl flooring, including SPC, LVT and vinyl planks, is commonly installed in HDB flats, condominiums and landed homes.
Is vinyl flooring suitable for commercial spaces?
Yes, vinyl flooring is used in offices, retail spaces, showrooms and other commercial environments, with the product chosen to suit expected foot traffic and use.
How should vinyl flooring be maintained after installation?
Regular cleaning, prompt removal of dirt and spills, and following the manufacturer's recommendations for the specific product will help keep a vinyl floor in good condition.
