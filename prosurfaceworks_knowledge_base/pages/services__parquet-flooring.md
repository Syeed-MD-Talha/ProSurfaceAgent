A dull or faded finish that no longer catches the light
Scratches or surface marks that stand out under closer look
Worn patches in high-traffic areas like hallways or living rooms
Varnish that's peeling, yellowing, or flaking off

CHOOSING THE TREATMENT
What Does Your Parquet Floor Need?
Polishing improves the surface appearance and finish where the floor is otherwise in good shape.
Sanding is more substantial preparation, used when the existing finish or surface needs to come back to bare wood.
Varnishing follows sanding, adding the protective and visual finish once the surface has been prepared.
The appropriate treatment depends on the parquet's condition, its existing finish, and the result you want to achieve.

LOCAL CONTEXT
Parquet Flooring in Singapore
Timber moves with changes in humidity and temperature, and Singapore's mix of air-conditioned rooms and open, humid corridors gives parquet more of that movement to deal with than it would see in a drier, more stable climate. Over time, that can affect how boards sit and how long a finish lasts, which is part of why parquet care here is assessed on the actual floor rather than a fixed timeline.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Can an old parquet floor be restored?
In most cases, yes. Whether it needs polishing, sanding and refinishing, or repair for damaged planks depends on the condition of the floor, confirmed during an on-site assessment.
Should parquet flooring be polished or sanded?
Polishing suits a floor that's simply lost its shine. Sanding is for floors where the existing finish or surface needs to be taken back before refinishing. The team can confirm which applies to yours.
How often does parquet flooring need refinishing?
This depends on foot traffic, sunlight exposure, and how the floor has been maintained, not a fixed interval. Heavily used areas typically need attention sooner than quieter rooms.
Can damaged parquet blocks be repaired?
Yes. Warped, loose, cracked, or missing planks can usually be repaired individually, without refinishing the entire floor, depending on how widespread the damage is.
Can old varnish be removed before refinishing?
Yes. Where the existing varnish has peeled, yellowed, or failed, it's stripped back first, since sanding and varnishing over a failed finish won't give a lasting result.
Can parquet flooring be installed in Singapore homes?
Yes. Parquet is installed in HDB flats, condominiums, and landed homes across Singapore, with the subfloor preparation and material choice suited to the space and how it's used.
