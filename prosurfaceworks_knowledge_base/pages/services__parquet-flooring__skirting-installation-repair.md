FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is floor skirting?
Floor skirting is the trim fitted along the junction between the floor and the wall, finishing that edge as part of a room's overall interior appearance.
When should skirting be repaired instead of replaced?
Repair is generally suitable when damage is localised and the existing skirting is otherwise sound. Replacement is considered when damage is extensive or the material no longer matches the flooring.
Can new skirting match existing parquet flooring?
In most cases, yes, though a perfect match cannot always be guaranteed on an older floor, particularly where the existing flooring has aged or faded unevenly. This is confirmed during assessment.
Can damaged skirting be repaired?
Many localised issues, such as loose sections, cracks or minor damage, can be repaired. More extensive or widespread damage may call for replacement instead.
Can skirting be installed during parquet flooring work?
Yes, skirting work is often coordinated with flooring installation or renovation. See
Parquet Flooring Installation
for new flooring work.
How long does skirting installation take?
This depends on the area involved and the extent of the work required, and is confirmed after assessment.
What type of skirting is suitable for parquet flooring?
This depends on the flooring type, the existing interior and your own preferences for material, colour and profile, and is discussed as part of the assessment.
