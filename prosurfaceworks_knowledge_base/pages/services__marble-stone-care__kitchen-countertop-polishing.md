When Does a Kitchen Countertop Need Polishing?
The surface has lost its original shine
The countertop looks dull compared to when it was installed
Light marks or fine scratches have appeared with everyday use
The finish looks uneven across the surface
Regular cleaning no longer brings back the appearance it used to
These signs generally point to surface-level wear from daily kitchen use, which polishing is designed to address.
What Can Kitchen Countertop Polishing Improve?
Surface shine and reflectiveness
Light surface wear from everyday use
Smoothness across the countertop
Consistency of finish from one section to another
Polishing is primarily a surface treatment and may not resolve deep chips, cracks or significant damage. Where the countertop shows more than surface wear, a different approach is usually needed.
Does Your Countertop Need Polishing or Restoration?
Polishing may be suitable when:
The surface is generally sound
The main concern is dullness
Wear is relatively light
The countertop simply needs its finish refreshed
Restoration may be more appropriate when:
There are deep scratches
Etching is significant
The countertop has chips or cracks
Staining is persistent
Surface damage is more extensive

Common Problems With Marble Kitchen Countertops
Dull patches around frequently used zones, such as near the sink or stove
Light scratches from utensils, cookware or everyday handling
Etching from acidic substances such as citrus, vinegar or wine
Water marks left by standing moisture
Staining from spills that were not cleaned up promptly
Loss of a consistent finish across the countertop
Surface wear concentrated around food preparation areas
Why Kitchen Use Can Affect Marble Surfaces
Kitchen countertops experience a different type of surface exposure from flooring. Food preparation, spills, acidic ingredients and frequent wiping all bring the surface into regular contact with substances and cleaning products, and heat from cookware placed directly on the surface can also play a role.
This does not mean marble is unsuitable for kitchens. It means the surface benefits from periodic professional attention to address the wear that comes with regular use.

Choosing the Right Countertop Finish
Marble countertops can be polished to different finish levels, and the suitable choice depends on the existing finish, how the kitchen is used and the client's preference.
A high-gloss finish for a brighter, more reflective surface
A lower-sheen or satin finish for a softer appearance
Neither finish is automatically the better option. The right choice depends on the countertop and what the client wants from it.
Marble Kitchen Countertops in Singapore
Kitchens in Singapore properties see frequent daily use, and marble countertops are regularly exposed to food and liquid spills, cleaning routines and everyday surface contact. Combined with typical indoor humidity, this contributes to the gradual dulling that polishing addresses.
How to Care for a Polished Marble Countertop
Clean spills promptly, especially acidic substances
Use stone-safe, pH-neutral cleaning products
Avoid acidic or abrasive cleaners not suited to marble
Use a chopping board or trivet during food preparation to protect the surface
Avoid abrasive scrubbing pads or scouring methods

Frequently Asked Questions
Can a dull marble kitchen countertop be polished?
Yes, dull countertops are one of the most common reasons clients request polishing, and the finish can generally be refreshed with the right treatment.
Can polishing remove scratches from a marble countertop?
Light surface scratches may improve through polishing, while deeper scratches or chips may require a more extensive restoration approach.
Can polishing remove etching from acidic spills?
Light etching often responds well to polishing. More significant etching may need a restoration-level treatment instead.
Does a marble countertop need restoration instead of polishing?
If the damage is limited to dullness or light wear, polishing is usually enough. For chips, cracks or deeper damage, see
Marble Restoration
How often should a marble kitchen countertop be polished?
This depends on how the kitchen is used and how the surface has been maintained. Our team can advise after assessing the countertop.
Can all marble countertops be polished?
Most marble countertops can be polished, though the suitable approach depends on the marble type and its current condition, which is confirmed during assessment.
