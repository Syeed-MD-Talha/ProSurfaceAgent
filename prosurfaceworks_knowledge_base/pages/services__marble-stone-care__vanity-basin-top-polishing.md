When Does a Vanity or Basin Top Need Polishing?
The surface looks dull or cloudy instead of reflective
The vanity top has lost the shine it had when installed
Water marks have built up around the basin
Light surface wear is visible around frequently used areas

These signs are typically the result of everyday bathroom use rather than significant damage.

How Everyday Bathroom Use Can Affect Marble
Vanity and basin tops are regularly exposed to water, mineral deposits, cleaning products and personal-care products, along with frequent wiping and everyday surface contact around the basin.
Repeated exposure to certain substances or unsuitable cleaning products can affect the appearance of some marble surfaces over time. This is a normal part of bathroom use, and periodic professional polishing helps keep the surface looking its best.
Choosing a Finish for Your Vanity Top
The right finish for a vanity top depends on the existing finish, how the bathroom is used, and what the client wants to achieve.
A high-gloss finish for a brighter, more reflective look
A softer, lower-sheen finish for a more understated appearance
Neither option is automatically the better choice, and maintenance considerations also factor into the decision.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can a dull marble vanity top be polished?
Yes, dullness is one of the most common reasons clients request vanity top polishing, and the shine can generally be refreshed with the right treatment.
Can water marks be removed from a marble vanity top?
Some surface water marks or deposits may improve with appropriate cleaning or polishing, but the required treatment depends on what caused the mark and how deeply it has affected the surface.
Can polishing remove scratches from a basin top?
Light scratches often improve through polishing. Deeper scratches may need a restoration-level treatment instead.
What is the difference between polishing and marble restoration?
Polishing addresses surface-level dullness and light wear. Restoration addresses deeper damage such as chips, cracks or significant etching. See
Marble Restoration
for more.
How often should a marble vanity top be polished?
This depends on how the bathroom is used and how the surface has been maintained. Our team can advise after assessing the top.
Can all marble vanity tops be polished?
Most marble vanity tops can be polished, though the suitable approach depends on the marble type and its current condition, confirmed during assessment.
