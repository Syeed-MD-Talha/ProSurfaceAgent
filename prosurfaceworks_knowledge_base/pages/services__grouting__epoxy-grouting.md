What Is Epoxy Grouting?
Epoxy grouting uses an epoxy-based grout system applied to the joints between tiles or marble. It has different characteristics from conventional cement-based grout, which affects where and how it is typically used.
The specific product and formulation used depends on the application and the material being grouted.
Why Choose Epoxy Grouting?
Resistance to staining in suitable applications
Easier cleaning in areas prone to dirt or residue build-up
Durability suited to certain demanding applications
Suitability for moisture-exposed areas
A consistent finish across the joints
These characteristics depend on the specific product used and how it is applied. Epoxy grout is not a 100% waterproof or permanent solution to every grouting issue, and results vary by application.

Epoxy Grouting or Regrouting?
Regrouting involves removing existing, deteriorated grout and replacing it with new grout. Epoxy grouting refers to a specific grout system or material. These are not always mutually exclusive concepts. When existing grout is removed and replaced, epoxy can be one of the grout options used.

Epoxy Grouting or Marble Gum Grouting?
Epoxy grouting is primarily about grout performance and application requirements. Marble gum grouting is primarily about creating a more seamless, marble-like floor appearance.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is epoxy grouting?
Epoxy grouting is the use of a resin-based grout system in the joints between tiles or marble, offering different characteristics from conventional cement-based grout.
Is epoxy grout suitable for bathrooms?
Bathrooms and other wet areas are common applications for epoxy grout, though suitability is confirmed for each project during assessment.
Is epoxy grout better than conventional grout?
Epoxy grout is not automatically better in every case. It has different characteristics suited to certain applications, while conventional grout remains a common and suitable choice for many standard uses.
What is the difference between epoxy grouting and regrouting?
Regrouting describes removing and replacing existing grout. Epoxy grouting describes a specific grout material, which can be used as part of a regrouting job. See
Tile & Marble Regrouting
for more.
Can epoxy grout fix leaks?
Epoxy grouting may help maintain grout joints in appropriate applications, but it should not be presented as a substitute for repairing an underlying waterproofing or plumbing problem.
How long does epoxy grouting take?
This depends on the area involved and the condition of the existing joints, and is confirmed after the assessment.
