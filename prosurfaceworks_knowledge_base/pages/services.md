# Our Services | Floor & Surface Restoration Singapore | Pro Surface Works

Our Services | Floor & Surface Restoration Singapore | Pro Surface Works

Services
Floor, Surface & Flooring Services
Pro Surface Works handles floor, marble, stone, grout, parquet, vinyl, and outdoor decking work across Singapore, for HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes, and commercial spaces. The work is organised into seven categories below, each covering a specific surface or type of problem rather than one broad flooring service.
The overview below gives a quick sense of what each category covers. For the full detail on a specific problem or surface, follow the link through to that service's own page.
SERVICE CATEGORIES
Our Service Categories
01
Marble & Stone Care
Marble and natural stone floors, countertops, and vanity tops lose their shine for different reasons. This category covers polishing for floors that have simply dulled with use, restoration for cracks and etching, and countertop or vanity top polishing for kitchens and bathrooms.
Marble Floor Polishing
Marble Restoration
Kitchen Countertop Polishing

02
Grouting Services
Grout lines often wear out, stain, or need upgrading well before the tiles around them do. This category covers standard cement-based regrouting, marble gum grouting for a seamless, glossy joint on homogeneous or porcelain tiles, and waterproof epoxy grouting for wet areas and new installations.
Tile & Marble Regrouting
Marble Gum Grouting
Epoxy Grouting
Explore Grouting Services
03
General Floor Care
Non-marble hard floors such as homogeneous tile, granite, terrazzo, and concrete need their own polishing and refinishing approach. This category covers routine polishing and buffing alongside deeper refinishing and restoration for floors that have lost their surface finish.
Floor Polishing & Buffing
Floor Refinishing & Restoration

04
Parquet Flooring
Parquet is particularly responsive to changes in Singapore's humidity, which is why it requires a different approach to maintenance and restoration. This category covers polishing, sanding and varnishing, repair, staircase refinishing, installation, old varnish removal, and skirting work.
Parquet Flooring Installation
Parquet Floor Polishing
Parquet Repair & Restoration

Old Varnish Removal
Skirting Installation & Repair
Explore Parquet Flooring
05
Timber & Wood Repair
Doors, furniture, cabinets, and window frames sometimes need the same kind of restoration and revarnishing as a floor, without being flooring at all. This category keeps that non-floor timber work separate.
Explore Timber & Wood Repair
06
Wood Decking
Outdoor timber decking on balconies, patios, and landed properties faces direct weather exposure, which calls for a different installation and repair approach than indoor timber.

Vinyl Flooring
This category covers SPC and LVT flooring guidance and professional installation.

OUR PROCESS
How We Help
Every job starts the same way, regardless of which category it falls under.
Assess: the surface is examined on-site to identify what's actually causing the problem, not just how it looks.
Recommend: based on that assessment, the appropriate service is recommended, whether that's polishing, restoration, repair, regrouting, or installation.
Complete: the in-house team carries out the work, from the first visit through to the finished result.
