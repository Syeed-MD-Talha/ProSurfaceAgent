THE SINGAPORE FACTOR
Why Singapore Conditions Affect Floor Care
Singapore's climate plays a real role in how floors here need to be maintained.
Humidity and heat: Marble stays cool underfoot, which is part of why it's popular here, but the same porous stone is sensitive to moisture and acidic spills, so Singapore's humid climate makes regular sealing and careful cleaning more important.
Timber movement: Parquet expands and contracts as humidity shifts between air-conditioned rooms and open corridors, which can loosen boards and wear the varnish down over time.
Grit from daily traffic: Fine grit tracked in from HDB corridors, condo car parks, and lift lobbies acts like sandpaper on tiled and marble floors underfoot.
DECISION GUIDE
Restore, Repair, or Replace?
A starting point before booking. The right option is confirmed during the free on-site assessment.
Dull but structurally sound: polishing or refinishing usually brings back the shine.
Etching, scratches, or stains: restoration addresses the damage without replacing the material.
A few loose, cracked, or gapped boards or tiles: localised repair is often enough.
Widespread structural or material failure: replacement may be the more practical option, confirmed on-site.
