What Is Parquet Flooring?
Parquet flooring is made up of individual wood pieces arranged into a pattern, rather than long continuous planks. Different patterns and designs are available, and parquet is used in both residential and suitable commercial spaces.
The individual pieces are what give parquet its distinctive look, and the way they are arranged is what sets one pattern apart from another. This also means installation involves more layout planning than laying down continuous plank flooring.

Parquet Flooring Installation in Singapore
Parquet flooring is installed in HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes, offices and other suitable properties across Singapore. The right installation approach depends on the specific property and space.
Existing conditions vary considerably between an older HDB unit, a newer condominium and a commercial office fit-out, which is another reason a site assessment is carried out before any installation plan is finalised.
