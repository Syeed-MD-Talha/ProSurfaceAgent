What Is Marble Gum Grouting?
Marble gum grouting is a treatment applied to the joints of an existing floor, using a gum compound to fill the grout lines before the surface is levelled and polished.
Treating the existing grout and joints
Filling the joints with a marble gum compound
Levelling and grinding the surface
Final polishing to bring out a consistent finish
The result is a floor with less visible grout lines and a more continuous, polished appearance. The exact material and process depend on the floor being treated.
Is Marble Gum Grouting Suitable for Your Floor?
Marble gum grouting may be worth considering for:
Existing tiled floors with visible grout lines
Homogeneous tile floors
Certain marble or stone surfaces
Floors where a more continuous visual appearance is desired
Floors where the existing grout lines are visually prominent
Suitability depends on the existing tile or material, its condition and the finish you want. Not every floor is automatically a good candidate, which is why an assessment comes before any recommendation.
Why Do Homeowners Choose Marble Gum Grouting?
Grout lines that have become prominent or visually distracting
Dark or dirty-looking joints that are hard to keep clean
An uneven visual appearance across the floor
A desire for a more seamless-looking floor
Tiles that are still structurally usable but look visually dated
Marble gum grouting does not turn any floor into real marble. It can create a marble-like visual finish while retaining the existing floor underneath.
Marble Gum Grouting vs Traditional Regrouting
Traditional regrouting
Removes and replaces existing grout
Grout joints remain visible after the work
Primary objective is repairing or renewing deteriorated grout
Marble gum grouting
Treats existing joints with a gum compound
Surface is levelled and polished
Objective includes creating a more continuous visual finish

Marble Gum Grouting or Epoxy Grouting?
Marble gum grouting is primarily focused on a seamless, marble-like visual finish. Epoxy grouting is primarily focused on grout-joint performance, such as durability and resistance suited to specific applications.

Our Marble Gum Grouting Process
1. Floor Assessment

2. Existing Grout Removal

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is marble gum grouting?
Marble gum grouting is a treatment applied to the joints of an existing floor to reduce visible grout lines and create a more continuous, marble-like polished appearance.
Can marble gum grouting be applied to homogeneous tiles?
Yes, homogeneous tile floors are one of the common surfaces considered for marble gum grouting, subject to an assessment of the specific floor.
Does marble gum grouting remove visible grout lines?
It significantly reduces how visible the grout lines are by filling and levelling the joints, though the result depends on the existing floor and material used.
What is the difference between marble gum grouting and regrouting?
Regrouting replaces deteriorated grout while keeping the joints visible. Marble gum grouting treats the joints to create a more seamless appearance. See
Tile & Marble Regrouting
for the repair-focused option.
What is the difference between marble gum and epoxy grouting?
Marble gum grouting focuses on visual finish. Epoxy grouting focuses on joint durability and resistance for specific applications. See
Epoxy Grouting
Can marble gum grouting be applied to every type of tile?
No, suitability depends on the tile or material type and its condition, which is why an assessment is carried out before any work begins.
