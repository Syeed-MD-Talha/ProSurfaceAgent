What Is Floor Refinishing & Restoration?
Assessing the existing floor's condition
Determining the appropriate treatment for a worn or deteriorated surface
Preparing the surface for treatment
Carrying out refinishing or restoration work
Bringing the floor back to an appropriate finish
Refinishing and restoration are not always the same treatment. Which one is appropriate depends on the floor's material, condition and the extent of the damage present.
When Should You Consider Floor Refinishing or Restoration?
Significant surface wear across the floor
Deep scratches that go beyond the surface layer
A dull or heavily deteriorated finish
An uneven appearance across the floor
Extensive surface damage
Deterioration of a previous finish or coating
The floor's overall appearance has substantially degraded

Floor Polishing, Refinishing or Restoration?
Polishing / Buffing
Generally suited to a surface appearance refresh, loss of shine, or lighter surface wear.
Refinishing
Focused on a more substantial renewal of the existing finish, including surface preparation and a new or renewed finish where appropriate.
Restoration
Focused on more extensive deterioration, correcting damage and bringing the surface back to a more serviceable or desired condition.

Floor Refinishing & Restoration in Singapore
High-use floors in Singapore homes and commercial properties, from residential living areas to busy commercial spaces, can show significant wear over years of everyday use, particularly where regular maintenance has lapsed. Refinishing or restoration is one way to bring these floors back to a usable, presentable condition.
When Does a Floor Need Replacement or Other Repairs?
Restoration may not be the appropriate solution where there is:
Severe structural damage
Major substrate failure
Extensive loose flooring
Serious water damage
Damage beyond what restoration can practically address
An on-site assessment is the appropriate way to determine whether restoration, replacement or another repair is the right path for your floor.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between floor refinishing and restoration?
Refinishing focuses on renewing the existing finish, while restoration addresses more extensive deterioration and damage correction. The right approach depends on the floor's condition.
How do I know if my floor needs polishing or refinishing?
If the floor is dull but structurally sound, Floor Polishing & Buffing is usually enough. Significant wear or damage points toward refinishing or restoration instead.
Can deep scratches be removed through floor restoration?
Many deep scratches can be improved through restoration, though the result depends on the depth of the scratch and the floor's material and condition.
Can every type of floor be refinished?
Most floor types within our service scope can be refinished or restored, though the suitable approach depends on the specific material and its condition, confirmed during assessment.
How long does floor restoration take?
This depends on the size of the area and the extent of the damage, and is confirmed after the assessment.
Does floor restoration mean replacing the floor?
No, restoration works with the existing floor rather than replacing it. Where damage is beyond practical restoration, replacement may need to be considered separately.
