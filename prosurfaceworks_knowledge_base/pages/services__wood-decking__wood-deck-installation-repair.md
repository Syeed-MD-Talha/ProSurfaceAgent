What Is Wood Decking?
Wood decking is an outdoor flooring surface built from timber boards, typically used to create a usable outdoor space with a natural material appearance.
It is commonly installed to extend a home or commercial property's living space into the outdoors, whether as a standalone feature or as part of a wider outdoor renovation.

Should You Repair or Replace Your Wood Deck?
Repair may make sense when:
The damage is localised
Most boards remain in good condition
The supporting structure remains sound
Only certain sections require attention
Replacement may be considered when:
The damage is widespread
Large areas are deteriorated
The supporting structure has serious issues
Repeated repairs are no longer practical
Neither approach is automatically the better option. The right decision depends on the specific condition of your deck, confirmed during assessment.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is wood deck installation?
Wood deck installation is the process of building a new outdoor timber deck, from site assessment and material selection through to board installation and finishing.
Can damaged wood decking be repaired?
In many cases, yes, particularly where the damage is localised. More extensive or widespread damage may call for more significant work, which is confirmed during assessment.
When should a wood deck be repaired instead of replaced?
Repair is generally suitable when damage is localised and the supporting structure remains sound. Replacement is considered when damage is widespread or repeated repairs are no longer practical.
What causes wood decking to deteriorate?
Outdoor exposure, including moisture, rainfall and sunlight, can cause weathering, discolouration, cracking, warping and, in some conditions, biological deterioration over time.
Can wood decking be installed on a balcony?
Yes, balconies are one of the common spaces where wood decking is installed, subject to the specific layout and conditions of the space.
How is a wood deck installed?
Installation involves assessing the site, planning the layout, selecting suitable material, preparing the base where applicable, installing and aligning the boards, and finishing the completed deck.
How is damaged decking repaired?
Repair involves assessing the extent of the damage, then addressing or replacing the affected boards or sections before finishing the repaired area.
Can you replace only damaged deck boards?
Yes, individual damaged boards can often be replaced without redoing the entire deck, depending on the extent of the damage and the availability of matching material.
